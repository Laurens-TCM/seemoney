    Update the data

    Export all accounts from Frollo as CSV and add the file here. It’s read on this device; the summary and spending lines are saved to the dashboard so trips can be matched.

    Built-in fixes: $950 loan repayments and card payments are treated as transfers, home loan interest counts as housing, TCM pay counts as income, the $45k TCM loan and the solar install are listed separately. Trips keep their ticked transactions when you upload a newer export.
